# Business Insights

## 1. Revenue Performance

The analyzed Olist dataset generated approximately 13.59M in revenue
across approximately 99K orders.

Revenue was concentrated among several leading product categories,
with Beauty & Health, Watches & Gifts, and Bed/Bath/Table among the
highest-revenue categories.

## 2. Customer Behavior

Approximately 96K customers were analyzed.

Repeat customers represented approximately 3.1% of the analyzed
customer base, indicating that most customers placed only one order.

This suggests an opportunity to investigate customer retention and
repeat-purchase strategies.

## 3. Geographic Performance

São Paulo generated the highest revenue contribution among the
analyzed states, followed by Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais.

These high-volume markets can be prioritized when analyzing logistics,
customer demand and regional performance.

## 4. Delivery Performance

Approximately 92% of delivered orders were classified as on-time,
while approximately 8K orders were identified as late.

Delivery performance varied across states, highlighting opportunities
for regional logistics analysis.

## 5. Customer Satisfaction

On-time orders had an average review score of approximately 4.3,
while late orders had an average review score of approximately 2.6.

This shows a substantial difference in customer ratings between
on-time and late deliveries in the analyzed dataset.

## 6. Business Implications

The analysis suggests several areas for further investigation:

- Identify the causes of late deliveries in high-volume states.
- Investigate factors influencing repeat purchases.
- Monitor high-revenue product categories.
- Compare seller and regional delivery performance.
- Track delivery performance alongside customer review scores.
